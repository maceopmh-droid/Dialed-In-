Help

Dialed In · a place to log your coffee and dial it in with friends

Questions, a bug, or something that needs a person? Email dialedincoffeeapp@gmail.com. We read everything and answer within a couple of days.

Signing in

Type your email and we send a 6-digit code (no password). No code? Check spam, wait a minute, then tap Send a new code.

Who sees what

Each session is for Everyone, Friends & groups, or Only me, and notes and photos can be kept back separately. You can change it any time, and make your profile private so you approve who follows you.

Something wrong in the feed

Report it from the ••• menu (sessions and comments) or the profile. You stop seeing it right away, nobody is told who reported it, and reports are reviewed within a day. You can also block someone from their profile.

Notifications

Settings, under your account, then Notifications on this phone. If the switch is greyed out, allow them in your phone's Settings, Notifications, Dialed In.

Your data and your account

Settings, under your account: Download my data gives you everything that's yours; Delete account removes your profile, sessions, photos, comments and likes for good.
