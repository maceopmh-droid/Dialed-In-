Your next-shot coach

Know exactly what to change next.

After every session, the coach reads your notes and numbers, holds them up against your best shot on that coffee, and gives you one clear move: grind finer, shorten the ratio, or repeat it exactly.

Reads what you wrote

Type “a little sour at times” or “less bitter than yesterday” and it gets it, hedges and all.

Aims for your best cup

Each shot is compared with your best one on that coffee, so every tip moves you toward a cup you already loved.

Speaks your grinder

It learns which way your grinder turns and how big one step is. It never borrows someone else’s grind number.

Catches the classic traps

A long ratio hiding sourness, a fast bitter shot that channeled (water rushing through cracks in the coffee), or beans speeding up as they age.

Questions

What is Dialed In?

Dialed In is a social coffee log app for people who brew at home. Log espresso, pour over, French press, AeroPress, moka pot and cold brew, see every cup as a 3D flavor shape, get a next-shot tip based on your own best brews, and share it all with your friends.

Is it free?

For now, yes. Accounts are free while Dialed In is in early access, so now’s the time to claim yours. There are no ads and no tracking.

Do I need an iPhone?

No. Dialed In runs in your web browser on any phone or computer, and you can add it to your home screen so it opens with one tap (the steps are just above). The iPhone app is coming soon to the App Store, and your account works in both.

Will a friend’s grind setting work on my grinder?

Usually not, because every grinder counts differently. That’s why the coach only ever uses your own grind numbers. Ratios, shot times and tastes do compare, so those are what you share and learn from.

Who can see what I log?

You pick for each session: everyone, your friends, or only you. You can also make your profile private and block anyone.

I’m new to brewing. Is it for me?

Yes. Log what you brewed and how it tasted, and the coach tells you what to try next in plain words. If you get stuck, ask the community.

How do I get help or report a problem?

Email dialedincoffeeapp@gmail.com or visit the support page. Sessions, comments and profiles can each be reported from inside the app.
