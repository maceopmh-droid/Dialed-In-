Terms of Use

Dialed In · last updated October 16, 2026

In short: Dialed In is a place to log your coffee and share it with friends and other coffee people. Be kind, keep it about coffee, and share only what's yours. We don't tolerate objectionable content or abusive people: anything reported is reviewed within a day, and people who break these terms are removed.

Who can use Dialed In

You need to be 13 or older. One account per person. You're responsible for what happens on your account, so keep access to your email safe.

What's yours

Your sessions, notes, photos and comments stay yours. When you share something, you let Dialed In store it and show it to the people you chose (Everyone, Friends & groups, or Only me). You can change who sees it or delete it at any time, and you can download everything or delete your account in Settings.

No tolerance for objectionable content or abuse

You agree not to post or send:

  • harassment, bullying, threats, or hate toward anyone, including for who they are
  • sexual content, violence, or anything meant to shock
  • spam, scams, ads or selling, or links meant to mislead
  • other people's photos or words without their permission, or anyone's private information
  • anything illegal, or anything that pretends to be someone else

Dialed In filters offensive words in names, comments and notes shared with Everyone, and holds links from brand-new accounts. Filters miss things, so please report what slips through.

Reporting and blocking

Every session, comment and profile can be reported (the ••• menu or Report). You stop seeing what you reported right away. Reports are reviewed within 24 hours; things that break these terms are removed, and the people who posted them can lose their account. When three different people report the same thing, it's hidden from everyone until it's reviewed. You can block anyone: you won't see each other, they can't find, follow or comment on you, and they aren't told.

Ending things

You can delete your account at any time in Settings; it removes your profile and everything in it. We can suspend or remove accounts that break these terms.

The fine print

Dialed In is provided as it is, without guarantees. Brewing advice in the app (including the next-shot coach and other people's answers) is a suggestion, not a promise. We're not responsible for what other people post, but we act on reports. We may update these terms; if a change matters, the app will tell you.
